About this lab

What is simplified

The building is a shear-building model. Each floor is one heavy concrete slab of 4,000 kg. Each story acts like a spring with damping between two slabs. When a story bends past its drift limit, its columns break into real physics pieces and the floors above fall.

The numbers are tuned to make the ideas clear. The bare frame has a natural beat near 1.2 Hz, the braced frame near 2.9 Hz, and the isolated building near 0.4 Hz on its bearings. Real tests record hundreds of sensors and are designed by engineers. Nothing here is a design tool.

Quake mode uses a synthetic earthquake built from many waves. It is not a recording of a real earthquake.
